Why quant-ml-protocol keeps letting you down, and what to replace it with

Review of ~/.claude/skills/quant-ml-protocol against our own six campaigns and 20+ public ML and quant repos. 2026-10-11. Proposed v2 draft: ~/.claude/docs/quant-ml-protocol-v2-draft-SKILL.md (not applied).
The skill fails because it is a 75 KB rulebook that guards against fooling ourselves and has nothing that drives discovery. Every working ML-research loop we read (Karpathy's autoresearch, AIDE, Numerai's own agent skill, MLEvolve, AI-Scientist-v2) works the other way round: a short program, the checks in a frozen evaluator written in code, a keep rule that accepts any real gain, coded stop-and-pivot triggers, and model-family breadth plus tuning as stages that cannot be skipped. The proposed v2 is 167 lines (8.9 KB) and keeps the validity core in code.

1. Why it failed: five causes

1
It defends against false positives and never against false negatives. The skill admits it: the September energy campaigns "ran valid, narrow and null" (SKILL.md:99-102). In prc26 campaign 1 a challenger beat the champion by +1.7 s, 95% CI [+1.1, +2.3] and was refused by FLOOR = 2.0 in taxi/verdict.py:8. Your reply: "wtf is refused by the floor?" None of the loops we read refuses a gain with a hand-set floor.
2
The rules live in prose, so the agent cannot hold them and every project hand-rolls them. 1,110 lines and 165 uses of never, must, always, only, mandatory, blocks or refused. btlab found three weaker hand-rolled nulls running at once. Almost every statistic the skill prescribes in prose is one tested library call: arch SPA, StepM, MCS and block bootstraps; skfolio and Nixtla walk-forward with purging; pypbo deflated Sharpe and PBO.
3
Each failure added rules, and the rules made the next failure more likely. SKILL.md grew from 8.3 KB (Aug 19) to 75 KB (Oct 3), and the folder from 16.5 KB to 287 KB. For example, missed tuning ("this is a key step in ML development, how can you miss it?", Sep 8) became "exactly 200 Optuna trials, rerun in every null world". That costs 160,000 trials per window configuration (SKILL.md:342-343), so tuning became too expensive to run again.
4
Its evals grade whether the agent recites the rules, not whether campaigns get better. The optuna-budget case passes only if the agent refuses 40 trials and insists on 200, even with about 60 independent observations. 34 train cases plus 11 held-out cases tuned the skill toward its own text.
5
It has no discovery engine. It has no error map, no oracle ceiling, no external scan of public repos and forums, no model-family scout and no plateau rule. Those are exactly what moved prc26: in the protocol-free days (Sep 12 to 14) the board went 310.891 to 271.783. After the protocol came back (instantiated in campaign 2 on Sep 18, commit e762f69), it sat between 270 and 274 until outside data found in a competitor's repo (ADS-B pushback times) took it to 255.6 and then 240.87 (prc26 LEDGER.md rows v21, v28, v53, v64, v72).

3. What the field does

A. A short program and a frozen evaluator causes 2, 3

autoresearch's whole instruction is program.md, 115 lines. The agent may edit only train.py; prepare.py (the evaluator) is read-only. Every run appends one row to results.tsv. AIDE's search policy is about 30 lines of code (aide/agent.py:162-193). Numerai's agent research flow is three files totalling about 17 KB (research 2.9 KB, experiment design 9.5 KB, AGENTS.md 4.5 KB, checked via gh api). Our skill folder is 287 KB.

github.com/karpathy/autoresearch, github.com/WecoAI/aideml, github.com/numerai/example-scripts (numerai/agents/)

B. A keep rule that accepts any real gain cause 1

autoresearch: "If val_bpb improved, you advance the branch". It adds a simplicity tie-break: "An improvement of ~0 but much simpler code? Keep." RD-Agent's quant loop: "Any small improvement should be considered for inclusion as SOTA". Noise control belongs in the stop rule, not the accept rule.

github.com/microsoft/RD-Agent rdagent/scenarios/qlib/

C. Coded plateau-and-pivot triggers cause 3 in alphanova, cause 5

Numerai runs rounds of 4 to 5 configs and stops after "at least two consecutive rounds" fail to beat the best (numerai-experiment-design/SKILL.md:13,32,51, read this session). MLEvolve marks a branch stagnant after 3 attempts that don't beat its own best, and stagnation switches the search to another branch or a fusion of two. Either rule would have stopped alphanova cycle 2's 30 variants of one ridge.

github.com/InternScience/MLEvolve (top open-source agent on MLE-bench, 61.33%)

D. Model-family breadth on day one, foundation models included cause 5, the TabPFN ask

AIDE writes 5 independent drafts before improving any. Every Kaggle winner we read blended families: Optiver 1st place (CatBoost, GRU and Transformer), Ubiquant 1st place (LightGBM and TabNet), NVIDIA's grandmasters (GBDT, NN, SVR, with TabPFN in the stack). Chronos-2, TimesFM and TabPFN-TS are each one call zero-shot. On financial returns, the evidence says expect near random walk; they are useful practical priors and cheap baselines (arXiv 2511.18578, 2606.27100, abstracts only). TabPFN-TS sends data to Prior Labs' cloud by default, so run it in LOCAL mode.

E. Tuning as a bounded stage that cannot be skipped causes 2, 3

AI-Scientist-v2 has a baseline_tuning stage that cannot close while the best node is still the base node. Numerai skips sweeps for feature changes and runs them for new architectures. NVIDIA's seven grandmaster techniques leave tuning out. mlforecast's AutoMLForecast makes the caller choose num_samples. No library fixes the trial count.

F. Validation that mirrors the test, calibrated once against external truth holdout missed the board

Optiver 1st place: "The CV score aligns with leaderboard score very well... So I just focus on improving CV". Jane Street 8th place: 2 folds of 200 dates, the size of the public test, plus a gap test against the private set. Ubiquant used two CV schemes and kept only changes that won on both. RD-Agent's feedback checks for an "Exact match between validation metric and official Kaggle metric". MLE-bench lists agents that used test-set feedback separately, as not comparable.

G. Mechanism and outside information beat search cause 5

Jane Street 8th place: online learning gave +0.008 against +0.001 for auxiliary targets. Ubiquant: one market-level aggregate lifted CV from 0.141 to 0.154. Optiver: post-processing read straight off the metric. RD-Agent mines Kaggle notebooks into an idea pool. Deotte's 2026 LLM-run win started from papers, forums and public code. None of the agent repos does error decomposition or oracle ceilings; that part of our prc26 recipe has no prior art in them and is worth keeping as our own.

H. Multiplicity from the logged search, not reruns causes 2, 3

No library reruns the whole search per null world. Every one handles multiplicity from the matrix of logged out-of-sample losses: SPA or StepM for "beats the champion after K tries", MCS for the tie set, deflated Sharpe with N set to the trial count for P&L. If every run is in the ledger, the search is countable and the bar rises as runs pile up.

5. The proposed v2

Draft: C:/Users/skf_s/.claude/docs/quant-ml-protocol-v2-draft-SKILL.md, 167 lines. Two files come first, before any model: a frozen evaluate.py (split mirroring the test, purge, paired interval, OOF predictions to disk) and an append-only results.tsv. Then six time-boxed stages:

0 Sources and errors~10%
External scan, naive baseline, error map, idea pool ranked by headroom
1 Draft breadth~10%
4+ model classes in parallel, one foundation-model slot
2 Tune~15%
One time-capped study on the top 1 or 2 drafts
3 Mechanism rounds~45%
4 to 5 variants per round; keep if CI > 0; stagnant after 3 runs or 2 rounds, then change construct
4 Ensemble~10%
Hill-climb or NNLS over every OOF file
5 Final read~10%
One untouched read, then the live ledger

Multiplicity: SPA, StepM or MCS on the logged loss matrix; deflated Sharpe with N = ledger rows for trading claims. Hard rules shrink to six lines, each backed by a test in the project.

What applying it also needs

  • Create references/audit.md from the heavy machinery being cut, so nothing is lost.
  • Trim references/model-families.md (it carries the 200-trial rules) and steering-bank.md to match.
  • Retire evals/ and evals-test/, and write 3 to 5 outcome evals from past campaigns.
  • Update the pinned hippo rule: "ten runs of one construct" becomes the v2 stagnation rule (3 runs or 2 rounds).
  • Later: a starter templates/evaluate.py and scripts/ledger.py (stagnation check read off results.tsv).

6. What we know, believe and have not tested

  • Know: the size growth, the floor refusal, the zero finished tuning studies by Sep 8, the eval design, the prc26 score path. Each is read from files or git this session.
  • Believe: the v2 loop would have found the prc26 levers sooner and stopped the alphanova sweep. Both are reasoned from the public loops, not measured.
  • Not tested: whether v2 kills false positives as well as v1 in a no-edge market like natgas. The first v2 campaign should run beside a v1-style audit on one claim to check.
